# Supermarket-Sales-Report-POWERBI-
Power BI dashboard analyzing supermarket sales: product line, city, customer type, payment etc
## 📊 Overview
This project is a Power BI dashboard analyzing supermarket sales data.  
It highlights key insights into **products, customers, cities, payments, and profitability**.

## 🔑 Features
- Products by Total Sales
- Sales by Product Line (Donut Chart)
- Revenue by City (Line Chart)
- Gross Margin % by Payment Type
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Customer Type Contribution

## 🛠 Skills Demonstrated
- DAX measures (`Total Sales`, `Product Rank by Sales`, `Gross Margin %`)
- Data modeling and Top N filtering
- Dashboard design and storytelling
- Business insights from raw data

  ## 📌 Insights
- Members contribute slightly more to sales than Normal customers  
- E‑wallets show higher gross margin % compared to cash and credit card  
- Top product lines drive ~85% of total revenue  
- Yangon leads in city‑wise revenue
